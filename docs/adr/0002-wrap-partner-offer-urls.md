# New Partner cards wrap offer URLs through our redirect

Telegram never tells us when a Tourist taps a URL button or caption link. New Partner cards send our redirect, then we send the Tourist on to the Partner’s referral URL, so we can count Referral Clicks. Messages already in chats keep the old direct URLs and stay uncounted.

We rejected counting Clicks only from the Partner’s own analytics: we would not have Opened View, and the Partner Dashboard would not be our funnel.
